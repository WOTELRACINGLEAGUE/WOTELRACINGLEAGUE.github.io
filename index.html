BRING THE THUNDER.

Join the WOTEL community and get ready for your next race night.

01

Connect with WOTEL

Join the Discord community for announcements, division information and help from Race Control.

02

Find your racing division

Ask staff which iRacing league and division to join. Confirm the current schedule and any entry requirements before signing up.

03

Get race-night ready

Read the league rules, check the required car and track, and review the event format. An iRacing subscription and the relevant content are required to race.

04

Take the green

Follow the event check-in instructions, join the session on time and listen to Race Control. Race hard. Race clean.
